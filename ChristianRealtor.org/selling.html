Common Questions

What Sellers Ask
Before They List

How long does it take to sell a home on the Gulf Coast?

+

Days on market varies significantly by neighborhood, price point, and time of year. Well-priced homes in high-demand communities like Waterset, FishHawk Ranch, and the Lakewood Ranch corridor have historically gone under contract in days. Luxury properties, rural acreage, and homes needing significant updates typically take longer. The single biggest factor is always initial pricing. Homes priced right from day one consistently outperform homes that start too high and end up chasing the market down.

Do I need to make repairs before listing?

+

Not always, and sometimes the answer is no. Luke walks through every home before it goes on the market and gives honest guidance on what is worth addressing and what is not. Some updates pay for themselves in a stronger offer price. Others cost more than they return. The goal is always to maximize your net proceeds, not to spend money for its own sake.

How do you determine the right list price?

+

Through a Comparative Market Analysis, which looks at recently sold homes comparable to yours in size, condition, location, and features. Luke does not use an algorithm. He pulls the actual comparable sales, walks the neighborhood, and builds a pricing recommendation around what the market will support for your specific property right now. The goal is a number that attracts motivated buyers and protects you from leaving money on the table.

What does your marketing include?

+

Professional photography, a personalized listing video shared on social media to drive attention beyond what the MLS alone reaches, full MLS exposure, and distribution across Zillow, Realtor.com, and other major platforms. Luke also leverages his network of active buyers and buyer agents who are currently searching in your market. Every listing gets the same level of attention regardless of price point.

What is the Florida Homestead Exemption and how does it affect my sale?

+

Florida's Homestead Exemption caps annual property tax assessment increases at 3 percent for your primary residence under the Save Our Homes provision. When you sell, the buyer loses your cap and is reassessed at current market value, which can significantly increase their annual property taxes. This matters in pricing strategy, especially in markets like Wesley Chapel and Riverview where assessed values have lagged behind rapid appreciation. Savvy buyers factor in the tax increase when making offers, and Luke walks every seller through this as part of every listing consultation. You can also model what buyers will face using the free property tax estimator on the tools page.

Should I be home during showings?

+

Generally, no. Buyers are more comfortable exploring a home and discussing it honestly when the seller is not present. Luke manages showing coordination so you receive advance notice, feedback after each showing, and regular updates on buyer activity. You stay informed without having to manage the logistics yourself.
