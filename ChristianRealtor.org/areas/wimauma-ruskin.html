More Possibilities. A Different Pace of Life.

Wimauma & Ruskin Real Estate

In southern Hillsborough County, Wimauma's growing planned communities and Ruskin's established neighborhoods, river frontage, and Tampa Bay shoreline offer different ways to feel at home. Neither fits a single label: Wimauma still has open, rural land, and most of Ruskin isn't on the water.

Wimauma vs. Ruskin

Two Communities, Different Choices

Both are unincorporated communities in Hillsborough County with the same county government and public school district. What differs is the age of the homes, the land, and how close you are to the water.

Hillsborough County · ZIP 33598

Wimauma

Historically farmland and rural homesites east of I-75, Wimauma has become one of the county's busiest areas for new master-planned communities along SR 674 and US-301, while still holding onto larger, rural properties.

  • You'll find: newer construction, amenity-rich planned communities, a gated 55+ community, and acreage outside the developments.
  • Trade-offs: CDD and HOA fees in most new communities, ongoing construction nearby, and growing traffic on SR 674.
  • Water: inland, with the Little Manatee River running through the area.
Hillsborough County · ZIP 33570

Ruskin

An older community between I-75 and Tampa Bay, with established neighborhoods, newer planned communities, homes along the Little Manatee River, and a stretch of bayfront around Bahia Beach.

  • You'll find: established homes, master-planned neighborhoods, river and bayfront properties, and marina living.
  • Trade-offs: coastal and riverfront homes carry higher flood and wind insurance, and older homes need careful inspection.
  • Water: some homes are on the river or bay; most Ruskin homes are not.

Six Communities, Six Different Choices

Wimauma · 55+

Valencia Lakes

An established, gated active-adult community with a clubhouse and resident amenities. A natural fit for downsizers and buyers relocating for retirement who want a resale home in a settled neighborhood.

Wimauma

Southshore Bay

A master-planned community built around a large, swimmable crystal-clear lagoon, with newer neighborhoods from several builders. Lagoon access and fees can vary, so confirm which amenities come with a specific home.

Wimauma

Berry Bay

A newer planned community with modern home designs and community amenities, offering a younger, all-ages contrast to the area's active-adult neighborhoods.

Ruskin

Belmont

A master-planned neighborhood with parks, a community amenity center, and a range of single-family homes, popular with buyers who want newer construction in Ruskin.

Ruskin

River Bend

An established residential community with neighborhood amenities and convenient access to US-41, I-75, and the rest of South Hillsborough.

Ruskin · Waterfront

Little Harbor & Coastal Ruskin

Condos and homes around the Bahia Beach marina area on Tampa Bay, plus scattered bayfront and riverfront properties. A very different lifestyle, and very different insurance costs, from inland neighborhoods.

The Local Lifestyle

Rivers, Mangroves, and the Bay

E.G. Simmons Conservation Park

A Hillsborough County park on Tampa Bay in Ruskin, with mangrove-lined waterways for paddling and fishing, a boat ramp, and waterfront camping. Park information.

Little Manatee River State Park

A state park in Wimauma with paddling on the Little Manatee River, miles of horse and multi-use trails, hiking, and camping. Trail closures happen, so check before you go. Official park guide.

Camp Bayou Outdoor Learning Center

A 160-acre nature preserve on the Little Manatee River in Ruskin, run by a volunteer nonprofit on county land, with trails, historic fishing cabins, and a fossil museum. Open limited days each week. Hours and details.

Buyer's Guide

Comparing Your Options in Wimauma & Ruskin

Many buyers here are weighing a new build against a resale home, or an all-ages community against a 55+ one. These are the questions that usually decide it.

  1. Builder incentives vs. the real price. Compare incentives, lot premiums, and upgrade costs against nearby resale homes. A lower base price isn't always the better deal.
  2. HOA and CDD fees. Most newer communities have both, and amounts vary by community and section. Check the tax bill for the specific address before you commit.
  3. 55+ rules. In a legally age-restricted community, residency rules can limit who may live in the home. Read the association's age and occupancy policies before you buy.
  4. Waterfront due diligence. For bay or river homes, inspect seawalls and docks, confirm water depth and access, and get flood and wind quotes. Our waterfront checklist covers more, and the waterfront homes guide compares coastal areas across the region.
  5. Acreage and rural lots. Check zoning, deed restrictions, wells, septic, and road access, especially outside the planned communities.
  6. Flood zone. Inland doesn't mean flood-free. Look up the flood zone for any home near the Little Manatee River, creeks, or low-lying land.

Buyer Questions

Wimauma & Ruskin: Common Questions

Both are unincorporated communities in Hillsborough County, so they share the same county government and public school district. Wimauma, east of I-75, has seen rapid growth in new master-planned communities while still having rural land and acreage. Ruskin, between I-75 and Tampa Bay, is older, with established neighborhoods, newer planned communities, and homes along the Little Manatee River and the bay. In general, Wimauma is the place to look for newer construction, and Ruskin offers more established homes and the area's waterfront options.

Several large master-planned communities in Wimauma are still building, so buyers can choose among builders, floor plans, and lots. Compare builder incentives, lot premiums, and upgrade costs against nearby resale homes, and check the HOA dues and community development district (CDD) assessment for the specific section, since CDD amounts can be significant in newer communities. Bring your agent on your first visit to a builder's model; many builders won't let an agent represent you if you registered without one. The new construction guide covers the process.

Yes, but most of Ruskin is not waterfront. Waterfront options include homes and condos around the Bahia Beach and Little Harbor area on Tampa Bay and properties along the Little Manatee River. Water depth, boat access, dock condition, and flood exposure vary widely from one property to the next, so confirm each one individually and get flood and wind insurance quotes during your inspection period. Many bay and riverfront homes are in FEMA high-risk flood zones; look up the address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center.

Most master-planned communities in both areas have an HOA, and many also have a community development district (CDD). HOA dues pay for amenities and common areas, while CDD assessments appear on the annual property tax bill and often fund roads, utilities, and amenities in newer communities. Fees vary widely by community and even by phase, and older Ruskin neighborhoods may have no HOA or CDD at all. Check the property tax bill on the Hillsborough County Tax Collector's website and ask for the HOA's current dues for the specific address.

Wimauma includes at least one legally age-restricted community, Valencia Lakes, but most of Wimauma is not a retirement area; many communities are open to buyers of any age. In a qualified 55+ community, federal law allows the association to require that most homes have at least one resident aged 55 or older, and the community's own rules may limit younger residents or long-term guests. Before buying, read the association's age-verification, occupancy, and rental policies, and confirm whether they fit your household's plans.
