Dade City vs. Zephyrhills

Two Small Cities, Plenty of Choice

Both are incorporated cities in eastern Pasco County, surrounded by unincorporated countryside. The differences are in their history, their housing mix, and how much new development is arriving nearby.

Pasco County · County Seat

Dade City

The seat of Pasco County, with a historic downtown around the old courthouse, the Church Street Historic District, and rolling countryside on every side. It's known for antique shops, local restaurants, and its annual Kumquat Festival.

  • You'll find: historic homes, established neighborhoods, golf community living, and acreage and estate properties in the surrounding hills.
  • Trade-offs: a longer drive to Tampa, and wells, septic, and older systems to evaluate on many properties.
  • Check: whether a home is inside city limits or in unincorporated Pasco County.
Pasco County · City of Zephyrhills

Zephyrhills

Known for its spring water and its skydiving airport, Zephyrhills combines an older downtown and established neighborhoods with newer subdivisions as growth spreads east from Wesley Chapel.

  • You'll find: established single-family neighborhoods, golf and lake communities, age-restricted and manufactured-home communities, and newer construction.
  • Trade-offs: CDD and HOA fees in newer communities, and more traffic as the area grows.
  • Nearby: Wesley Chapel to the west.

Six Ways to Live in East Pasco

Dade City · Historic

Historic Dade City & Church Street

Homes near the historic downtown, including the Church Street Historic District, listed on the National Register of Historic Places. Expect older homes with character, mature trees, and walkable streets close to shops and restaurants.

Dade City · Golf

Lake Jovita

A golf and country club community set in the hills south of Dade City, with larger homes and course views. Confirm HOA dues, club membership, and community rules for the specific home.

Dade City · Land

Greater Dade City Acreage & Estates

Homes on acreage, estates with room for horses or workshops, and custom homes in the rolling countryside around Dade City, with more privacy and space than a typical subdivision.

Zephyrhills

Historic Zephyrhills & Downtown

Older neighborhoods around the city's downtown and its landmark park, with established homes on traditional streets and an easy walk or short drive to local shops and events.

Zephyrhills · Golf

Silver Oaks

An established Zephyrhills neighborhood of single-family homes set around a golf course, with mature landscaping and a settled, residential feel.

Zephyrhills · New

Newer Zephyrhills Communities

New subdivisions with modern floor plans and community amenities are being built around Zephyrhills. Most have HOA dues and many have CDD assessments, so compare total monthly costs, not just price.

Acreage & Historic Homes

A Property With More Possibilities

Land and history are a big part of the appeal here, and both come with questions a typical subdivision home doesn't raise. Not every item applies to every property, but these are worth answering before you commit.

  1. Wells and water quality. Have the well, pump, and equipment inspected, and test the water.
  2. Septic systems. Inspect the system and ask about its age, size, and service history.
  3. Survey and boundaries. Order a current survey to confirm boundaries, fences, and any encroachments.
  4. Zoning, easements, and access. Confirm what the zoning allows, any easements across the land, and whether road access is public or private.
  5. Historic considerations. In Dade City, historic properties may be subject to review by the city's Historic Preservation Advisory Board before exterior changes.
  6. Insurance and older systems. Older homes often need a 4-point inspection for insurance. Check the roof, electrical, and plumbing early.

Buyer Questions

Dade City & Zephyrhills: Common Questions

Both are incorporated cities in eastern Pasco County surrounded by unincorporated countryside. Dade City is the county seat, with a historic downtown, the Church Street Historic District, and rolling countryside known for acreage and estate properties. Zephyrhills is larger, known for its spring water and skydiving airport, and combines an older downtown and established neighborhoods with newer subdivisions as growth spreads east from Wesley Chapel. In general, Dade City offers more historic character and land, while Zephyrhills offers more newer homes and everyday conveniences.

Yes. Much of the countryside around Dade City and Zephyrhills is made up of homes on acreage, small farms, and estate properties, some with room for horses, barns, or workshops. When you look at land, confirm the zoning and any deed restrictions, order a current survey, check easements and road access, inspect the well and septic system, and look up the flood zone on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center, especially near creeks and low-lying areas.

Beyond a standard home inspection, plan on a well inspection and water test, a septic inspection, and a current boundary survey. Older homes may need a 4-point inspection for insurance, and a wind mitigation report can lower premiums. If the property has a barn, workshop, pool, or other outbuildings, have those inspected too, and confirm that major structures and additions were permitted.

Dade City has a National Register historic district along Church Street and a local register of historic properties, and the city has a Historic Preservation Advisory Board that reviews certain changes to historic properties. Before you buy, ask whether the home is on the local register and what review applies to exterior changes. Plan for inspections of the roof, electrical, plumbing, and foundation, since older systems may need updating, and get insurance quotes early.
