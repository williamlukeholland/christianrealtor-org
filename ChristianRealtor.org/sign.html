Quick Answers

How do I schedule a showing for a home on one of Luke's signs?

Text or call William 'Luke' Holland at (813) 437-1742 with the address from the sign and the times that work for you. Luke will confirm a time and meet you at the home.

Is the home on the sign still available?

The homes on this page come straight from the MLS and only show if they're still active. If you don't see the home from the sign, it may be under contract or already rented. Text Luke and he'll tell you its status and help you find similar homes nearby.

Does Luke help with rentals as well as homes for sale?

Yes. Luke lists homes for sale and for rent across Tampa Bay and the Gulf Coast, from Dade City to Sarasota. Both are shown above.

What if I'm already working with a real estate agent?

That's no problem. Ask your agent to contact Luke at (813) 437-1742 and they can schedule the showing for you.
