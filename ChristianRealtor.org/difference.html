Meet Your Realtor

William "Luke" Holland

Tampa-raised. Economics-minded. Fully invested in the outcome.

William "Luke" Holland was born and primarily raised in Tampa, Florida, where he developed a deep appreciation for the communities he now proudly serves. Much of his childhood was spent traveling throughout the state playing competitive baseball — an experience that shaped the discipline, resilience, and team-first mindset he brings to every client relationship today. While the uniform has changed, his willingness to travel wherever his clients need him has not.

After retiring from collegiate baseball, Luke earned a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration with a concentration in Economics. It was through studying markets and wealth creation that he discovered a passion for real estate — not simply as buying and selling homes, but as one of the most powerful tools families have for creating long-term financial security and generational wealth.

Since entering the industry, Luke has earned recognition for his work ethic, attention to detail, and unwavering commitment to excellence. He believes every transaction deserves the same level of preparation, communication, and advocacy, whether helping a first-time buyer, guiding a family through a move, or negotiating on behalf of a seasoned investor.

For Luke, real estate is far more than a career. It is an opportunity to help people make informed decisions that strengthen their future, one home at a time.

Local Perspective

Hannah Lee Petitt

Seventh-generation Tampa native. Multigenerational real estate background. Educator at heart.

As a seventh-generation Tampa native with roots in the Alderman family, Tampa has always been home. Growing up here gave Hannah a deep appreciation for the people, neighborhoods, and history that make the Tampa Bay area such a special place to live.

Real estate has been part of her life for as long as she can remember. Her father, Richard Petitt, has practiced real estate, title, and construction law since 1991 and serves as President of Sunset Park Title Company. Through him, Hannah was introduced early to the legal, contractual, and title side of every real estate transaction.

On her mother's side, her grandparents, James "Jim" Lee and Ann Lee, founded Sabal Homes in 1971 with a simple mission: "From our family to yours." Some of Hannah's favorite childhood memories were spending Saturdays at model homes — watching homes being staged, sitting in color selection meetings, and seeing families get excited about building a place of their own. Those experiences taught her that buying a home is about so much more than square footage. It's about creating the place where life happens.

Her grandmother, Ann Lee, later opened Ann Lee Realty and has served the Tampa Bay community for more than fifty years. Growing up around builders, Realtors, title professionals, attorneys, buyers, and sellers gave Hannah a unique perspective on the home-buying process from nearly every angle.

Today, Hannah is a public school teacher, and that role has shaped the way she works with people just as much as her family's real estate background. Every day, she helps young learners build confidence by breaking big ideas into manageable steps, answering questions with patience, and creating an environment where people feel comfortable learning. She believes buying or selling a home should feel the same way — clients deserve someone who explains the process clearly, keeps them informed, and makes sure they never feel alone in one of life's biggest decisions.

At Christian Realtor, Hannah brings together generations of local real estate knowledge with the heart of an educator. Whether she's helping behind the scenes, creating resources, or supporting clients throughout the process, her goal is simple: to help every family feel informed, cared for, and confident from beginning to end.
