Step by Step

What Buying a Home
Actually Looks Like

Every buyer's journey is a little different. This is the general shape of it, with honest notes on what matters at each stage.

1

Share What You're Looking For

The process starts when you tell Luke your priorities: area, price range, must-haves, and what matters most. Luke reviews your criteria personally and sends you a curated list of Gulf Coast homes worth considering, including resale listings, new construction options, and neighborhoods you may not have considered yet.

No pressure, no pitch, no obligation. Just a locally informed starting point built around your life. Request your free home list to get started.

2

Get Pre-Approved

Before you start touring homes, connect with a lender to find out exactly what you can comfortably afford. Pre-approval tells you your real number, not a rough estimate, and it signals to sellers that you're a serious, ready buyer. In competitive markets like Wesley Chapel and Riverview, sellers will often not consider an offer without one.

Use the free Mortgage Calculator on the tools page to run a quick estimate before your lender conversation.

✦ Preferred lender: Luis Peguero, Fairway Independent Mortgage — loanswithluis.com
3

Build Your Customized Search

Armed with your pre-approval and the context from your first conversation, Luke builds a search strategy around what you actually need. That includes neighborhoods he recommends based on your criteria, communities worth knowing about that might not be on your radar yet, and filters that go beyond bedrooms and bathrooms.

When something matches, you hear about it. When something has a problem, Luke tells you before you waste a Saturday touring it.

4

Tour Homes Together

Luke attends every showing. He's not there to validate your excitement, he's there to give you an honest read on each property. What does the neighborhood feel like at 5pm? What's the roof look like? What is that smell? What does the comps data say about the asking price?

The goal is for you to walk away from each showing with more information, not more confusion.

5

Make a Competitive Offer

When you find the right home, Luke pulls the comparable sales, reads the market conditions for that specific neighborhood, and helps you craft an offer that is competitive without overpaying. He negotiates on your behalf with your priorities first, not his commission check.

He'll also walk you through every term before you sign so you know exactly what you're agreeing to.

6

Inspection and Due Diligence

Once your offer is accepted, an independent inspection is one of the most important steps in the process. Luke coordinates with trusted inspectors and walks you through the findings. He helps you understand what's a deal-breaker, what's negotiable, and what's normal for a home of that age and type.

The lender-ordered appraisal happens here too, confirming the home's value supports your loan amount.

✦ Preferred inspector: William Larson, Larson Inspections — larsoninspection.com
7

The Gap Between Contract and Closing

Most buyers don't realize how much happens in the 30 to 45 days between accepted offer and closing day. Title work, loan conditions, HOA estoppel letters, survey, final walk-through. Luke manages this timeline with you so nothing falls through the cracks and you aren't scrambling in the final week.

8

Closing Day

You sign the final documents, transfer funds, and get your keys. Luke will be there. He will have already walked you through what to expect so closing day feels like a celebration, not a surprise.

And when the door closes on that chapter, the relationship with Luke doesn't end. He remains a resource for as long as you need one.

Common Questions

What Buyers Ask
Before They Start

How long does it take to buy a home on the Gulf Coast?

+

From the first conversation to closing day, most buyers in the Tampa Bay and Gulf Coast area are under contract within two to eight weeks of active searching, depending on how competitive the market is in their target neighborhoods. Once under contract, closing typically takes 30 to 45 days with a financed purchase. Cash purchases can close faster. Timeline varies based on your flexibility, budget, and how specific your criteria are.

Do I need to be pre-approved before we start looking at homes?

+

Yes, and it protects you. Pre-approval tells you exactly what you can afford, which prevents heartbreak over homes outside your range, and it signals to sellers that you are a serious buyer. In competitive Gulf Coast markets like Wesley Chapel, Riverview, and Lakewood Ranch, sellers often will not consider an offer without a pre-approval letter. Luke works with Luis Peguero at Fairway Independent Mortgage and can make an introduction the same day.

What should I know about HOA and CDD fees in Florida?

+

Both affect your true monthly cost of ownership and are worth understanding before you fall in love with a community. HOA fees cover shared amenity maintenance and community rules. CDD fees are a separate line item on your property tax bill that fund infrastructure bonds in master-planned communities. They do not go away when the community is built out, and they vary significantly from one neighborhood to the next. Luke walks every buyer through these numbers before submitting any offer.

What is flood zone and how does it affect my purchase?

+

Florida's Gulf Coast has a range of flood zones, from minimal risk to mandatory flood insurance areas. A home in a high-risk flood zone can cost several thousand dollars a year more to insure than a comparable home outside that zone. Luke checks flood zone designation as part of evaluating any property and can connect you with insurance partners to get real numbers before you make a decision.

Can I buy a new construction home through Luke?

+

Yes, and it matters that you bring him before your first model home visit. Many builders require a Realtor to be registered on your very first visit in order to represent you through the purchase. Luke represents buyers at Waterset, Epperson, Lakewood Ranch, North River Ranch, and communities across the full Gulf Coast corridor. See the dedicated new construction page for more detail on how that process works.

Does working with a buyer's agent cost me anything?

+

In most transactions, the seller's side compensates the buyer's agent, meaning Luke's representation typically costs you nothing out of pocket. Compensation structures have evolved in recent years, and Luke will walk you through exactly how it works for any specific property before you are under any obligation. The guidance, negotiation, and advocacy you receive are a significant advantage regardless of how compensation flows.
